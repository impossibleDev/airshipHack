Airship controls

  • WASD Walk around the deck; at the pilot seat, W/S thrust and A/D turn.
  • E Use a nearby station or leave the one you are using.
  • At a turret, aim with the mouse and click to fire.
  • Select a build item (or press 1–3), point at an empty deck tile, then press F.
  • Repair a broken generator with E. Hold the capture point to score.

New and respawned crews have 5 seconds of spawn protection.

CREW DESTROYED

Game over

Your airship has been destroyed. Respawn with your crew to return to the battle.
